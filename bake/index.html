Automated discovery of closed-form analytical solutions to PDEs.

Law-Mind is designed to discover closed-form analytical solutions to partial differential equations directly from governing equations, without numerical data or supervision. The core workflow repeatedly constructs candidate solution functions from mathematical symbols, substitutes them into the target PDE, evaluates the residual, and retains only the candidates that pass symbolic verification.

Paper overview

Law-Mind addresses a narrow but consequential scientific problem: how to search for exact analytical solutions of PDEs when only the governing equation is available. Instead of relying on numerical supervision, sampled trajectories, or fitted coefficients from external data, the method searches directly in symbolic function space.

The algorithm decomposes candidate solutions into mathematical components, recombines these components into structured expressions, and uses symbolic residual evaluation as the acceptance criterion. This makes the workflow interpretable, mathematically grounded, and visually suitable for a state-machine style presentation.

Method summary

  • Fix a target PDE and expose its operator structure.
  • Generate candidate solution structures from reusable symbolic primitives.
  • Split each candidate into mathematical tokens or function parts.
  • Recombine the tokens into new closed-form hypotheses.
  • Substitute the hypothesis into the PDE and compute the residual.
  • Retain or reject the candidate, then continue the loop.
